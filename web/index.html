About the numbers. Values tagged datasheet come from vendor or distributor spec pages. Values tagged est. are planning estimates; edit them in each device card to match the current datasheet before you send a design to a customer. Edited values are saved with the project.

Camera reach is the farthest seat at which a face is framed usefully, not the lens's optical limit. Mic pickup uses the vendor's figure scaled by the planning radius slider, since rated pickup assumes a quiet, treated room. Display size uses the 6× rule: the farthest seat sits no more than six image-heights from the screen.

3D view. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Camera view shows the room through the device's lens at its horizontal field of view.
